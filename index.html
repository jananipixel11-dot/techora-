If you are lost
  • STOP: Stay, Think, Observe, Plan. Do not wander further.
  • Move to an open area, stay visible, avoid rivers and steep ground.
  • Signal in threes: 3 whistle blasts, 3 flashes, 3 shouts.
  • Send SMS instead of calling. It needs less signal.
  • Save battery: low brightness, close other apps, keep GPS on.
  • Never approach wildlife. Do not travel after dark.
